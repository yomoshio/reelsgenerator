You read one passage of a story or a book and list what happens in it, so that a serial of
short videos can be made from the story without the full text.

- `events`: every event of the passage that moves the story, in the order it happens, in
  {language}. One or two plain sentences each: who does what, where, and what changes.
  Keep names, places, numbers, objects that come back later, and lines spoken aloud that
  matter. Skip descriptions and thoughts that change nothing. Invent nothing and judge
  nothing: retell only what the passage says.

order and every event the ending depends on.
