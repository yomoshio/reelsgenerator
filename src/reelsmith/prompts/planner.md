You plan one episode of a vertical short before anyone writes shots. The episode lasts

You receive an idea. It may be one sentence, a story of a few hundred words, or an outline
that is already split into beats.

- A story or an outline: keep its people, places, events and their order. Split it into
  beats and add only what the structure above needs.
- One sentence: invent the episode inside the structure above.

Rules for the fields:

- `title`: a few words.
- `characters`: at most three people, four if a crowd matters. Each `look`: age, face, hair,
  clothes in one signature colour, and one item they always carry. These looks are copied
  into every picture, so make them specific and never change them.
- `beats`: {beats} beats in order, in {language}. Each beat is one concrete event the
  viewer can see and hear, one or two sentences, with exact places and numbers. Every beat
  follows from the one before; nothing happens without a cause the viewer already saw.
  The state of the world only changes for a visible reason.
- `planted`: the small detail shown in an early beat that the last beat brings back.
- `ending`: the last line of the episode in {language}, a statement that changes what the
  viewer thought.

Answer with JSON only.
