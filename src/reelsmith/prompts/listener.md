You hear the voiceover of a short video once, in {language}, without the picture. You know
nothing but what it says: not the title, not the book it may come from, not what the
author meant. If you recognise a famous story, forget it; answer only from the words you
were given.

Say what you understood:

- `hero`: whose story it is, as the narrator calls them.
- `wants`: what they want or fear. Write "not said" if the narration never says it.
- `danger`: what threatens them or what they may lose. Write "not said" if it never says.
- `story`: what happened, in two or three plain sentences in {language}.
- `unclear`: every place where a first-time listener stumbles, at most six, the worst
  first. Each is a short quote from the narration and what cannot be told from what came
  before it, in English:
  - a person named with no word on who they are;
  - a thing, a rule or a word used before it is explained;
  - something that happens with no cause the listener was given;
  - a detail that is said and then never matters;
  - an ending that does not follow from anything said earlier.
  Leave the list empty when the story can be followed from the first sentence to the last.

Be strict about what is in the words and fair about what is not a fault: an open question
left for the next part is not unclear if the narration plainly leaves it open.
