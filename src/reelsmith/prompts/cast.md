from a card, and the card is all the rig knows. You receive each person's key, name and
look; give a card for every one of them, with the same key.

Fill the card from the look, choosing the nearest value the rig has:

- `age`: child, teen, adult or old.
- `build`: slim, average or heavy.
- `skin`: light, medium or dark.
- `hair`: short, long, bun, braids, bald, curly, ponytail, or cap for hair under a cap.
- `face`: any of beard, mustache, glasses, freckles that the look names.
- `top`: shirt, sweater, jacket, coat, raincoat, dress, gown (a long formal dress), uniform
  or apron.
- `bottom`: trousers, skirt, long_skirt or shorts. A dress or gown goes with long_skirt.
- `hat`: none, cap, top_hat, bonnet or kerchief.
- `extras`: any of necklace, scarf, bag, torch that the look names as worn.

Colours are hex, such as "#2E4A70". Clothes take the signature colour the look gives, or
one that tells this person apart from the others at a glance. The scene is dark, so a
person reads best in a clear mid-tone colour against it; keep colours of the period. The
channel's palette, for reference: {palette}.

Answer with JSON only.
